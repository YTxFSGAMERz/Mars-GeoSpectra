# Hackathon Judge UX
## Goal
Make the value obvious quickly: integrated NASA data becomes a useful Marswalk planning workflow.
## First screen
Mars, project identity, one “Explore Jezero” action, and a concise purpose statement.
## Hierarchy
Primary: map/route. Secondary: layer controls/objective. Tertiary: provenance/methodology/details.
## Reliability
Preload the first scene, keep fixture/demo mode, and provide one-click sample objectives.
## Visual restraint
Avoid fake NASA branding or UI implying official operational status. Polish without pretending to be NASA software.
## Judge questions
Show what data is integrated, what decision is supported, what was computed, why a route was chosen, what evidence exists, and what remains uncertain.
